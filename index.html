VISTA 10/10 BOCCE · PILOT

10 POINTS.
10 FRAMES.

A Vista-developed tournament format built on USBF Open Bocce rules, with the tournament mechanics designed for efficient four-court play.

10/10First to 10 points or completion of 10 frames.
11Tied after 10? New coin toss, then one sudden-death frame.
SEEDWins first; then bracket head-to-head or cross-bracket differential rules.
12Championship may be first to 12 when announced before playoffs and time permits.
View pilot tournament mechanics

Bracket play: top three from each bracket are preferred qualifiers; field structure remains event-specific.

Seeding: cross-bracket Win/Loss → Point Differential → Points For.

Playoffs: single elimination, standard seeded placement, mathematical byes to highest seeds.

Operations: master game order, four-game On Deck queue, FIFO court flow, Tournament Control.

Equipment: USBF Open specifications and rules unless Vista 10/10 explicitly modifies the format.

Pilot: results preserved under the rules version played; player survey sent about 24 hours after the event.

THE HOME COURTS

BRENGLE TERRACE PARK

The four bocce courts at Brengle Terrace Park are central to Vista's bocce story. The courts were established with help from founders and members of the American Italian Club of Vista; current court renovation/improvement work is a City of Vista Parks & Recreation project.

Court availability shown here will be limited to Vista Bocce Club events unless the club receives access to City Parks & Recreation scheduling information.
