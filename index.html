Why Vista Bocce Club

A club with room for everyone.

Vista Bocce Club is being built around a simple idea: make it easy for people to discover bocce, find a game, improve their skills, compete, and become part of a local community.

We’re starting at Brengle Terrace Park and building toward organized open play, leagues, tournaments, instruction, community events, and a strong long-term home for bocce in Vista.
