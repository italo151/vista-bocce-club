The Club

Built for people who love to play.

Vista Bocce Club is being built to bring organized bocce, community events, instruction, leagues, and competitive play to Vista and the surrounding North County community.

Whether you have played for decades or have never rolled a bocce ball, you will have a place here.

Our Home

Brengle Terrace Park

The future home of Vista Bocce Club features four bocce courts in one of Vista’s best-known community parks. Our goal is to support the courts, improve the playing experience, and make them a gathering place for bocce in North County.
