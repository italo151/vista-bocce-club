VISTA 10/10 BOCCE

10 POINTS.
10 FRAMES.
EVERY POINT MATTERS.

A competitive bocce format where winning matters—and so does every point along the way.

Vista 10/10 brings a defined structure to tournament bocce while preserving the strategy, skill, and traditions of the game.

Reach 10 points first and you win. If neither team reaches 10, the team leading after 10 completed regulation frames wins. Tied after 10? Settle it on the court with one sudden-death frame.

THE FORMAT

SIMPLE TO UNDERSTAND.
BUILT FOR COMPETITION.

Vista 10/10 doesn't change the fundamentals of bocce. It gives tournament play a clear competitive structure.

Ten points can end the game. Ten frames guarantee it will reach a decision. And every point scored along the way can matter beyond the game you're playing.

10

REACH 10. WIN THE GAME.

The first team to reach 10 points wins immediately. No additional frames are played.

10

IF NOBODY REACHES 10, THE FRAMES DECIDE IT.

After the 10th completed regulation frame, the team with the higher score wins.

+1

TIED AFTER 10? SETTLE IT ON THE COURT.

Play one sudden-death 11th frame. A new coin toss determines pallino control and first ball. The frame winner receives one official point.

WHY EVERY POINT MATTERS

WIN THE GAME.
BUILD YOUR POSITION.

Winning the game is always the first objective. But when teams finish preliminary play with identical records, Vista 10/10 looks deeper into what happened on the court.

PRELIMINARY STANDINGS

PERFORMANCE BUILDS YOUR POSITION.

Every preliminary game contributes to the standings. Wins and losses come first. When records are tied, clearly defined competitive tiebreakers determine who advances.

01WIN / LOSS RECORDWinning comes first.
02HEAD-TO-HEAD RESULTUsed within a preliminary bracket.
03POINT DIFFERENTIALPoints For minus Points Against.
04POINTS FORTotal preliminary points scored.
05ONE-FRAME PLAYOFFSettle remaining ties on the court when practical.
06PALLINO LAG / SHOOTOUTUsed when an on-court playoff is not practical.

Forfeits: A forfeit records the win/loss result but does not artificially create Points For, Points Against, or point differential.

THREE GAMES. EVERY ONE COUNTS.

Each four-team preliminary bracket is a round robin. Every team plays the other three teams in its bracket once. The top three teams advance.

FOUR BRACKETS BECOME ONE FIELD.

Advancing gets you into the playoffs. Your overall preliminary performance determines where you are seeded.

YOUR BRACKET GETS YOU IN. YOUR PERFORMANCE DETERMINES WHERE YOU GO.

On Deck means ready to play—not assigned to a court. The next eligible matchup is assigned to the next appropriate available court according to the predetermined master game order. If a team is still playing, its matchup may be temporarily skipped without losing priority.

Each match uses one official scorecard visible to both teams. Where an official scorekeeper is assigned, that official maintains the scorecard. Otherwise, the Tournament Director establishes the event's scoring procedure.

OFFICIALS & DISPUTES

SETTLE THE GAME ON THE COURT.

An assigned referee is the primary authority during normal match play. The Tournament Director is the ultimate tournament authority.

Judgment calls generally stand. Questions involving rules, procedure, scoring, eligibility, or material interpretation may be appealed to the Tournament Director. Measurements are normally resolved by the players unless an official is assigned or intervention is required.

UNDERLYING RULES

TRADITIONAL BOCCE
AT ITS CORE.

Vista 10/10 uses USBF Open Bocce Rules as its underlying playing rules except where the published Vista 10/10 rules specifically modify tournament format, scoring, standings, seeding, or tournament procedure.

WE'RE NOT CHANGING THE GAME.
WE'RE CHANGING THE TOURNAMENT AROUND IT.

PILOT / TEST RULESET

BUILT TO BE TESTED.

Vista 10/10 begins as a Pilot / Test Ruleset. We intend to run it in real competition, collect tournament data, evaluate tournament flow, and ask registered players for structured feedback.

The objective isn't to defend the first version. The objective is to build a better tournament format.
